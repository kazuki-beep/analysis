Financing

Don't let CAPEX stop decarbonization.

Most decarbonization plans stall at the same place: a technically sound project with no capital behind it. Once emissions have been measured and projects have been identified and quoted, Bono can help connect that roadmap with financing opportunities — because a documented business case is what makes a project financeable.

  1. 01

    Carbon data

    A measured, auditable inventory.

  2. 02

    Project

    A defined technical scope.

  3. 03

    Business case

    CAPEX, savings, ROI, $/tCO₂.

  4. 04

    Financing

    Connected to financing opportunities.

  5. 05

    Implementation

    Executed with the selected provider.

  6. 06

    CO₂ reduction

    Tracked back into the platform.

The data is the collateral

Financial institutions ask for evidence. Measured emissions, a quoted project and a modelled return are exactly that.

Carbon and capital in one view

Every project carries both numbers: tonnes avoided and money returned. Sustainability and finance stop working from different files.

Built with the financial sector

Bono works alongside financial institutions in the region on the connection between decarbonization projects and sustainable finance.

Bono facilitates connections to financing opportunities. Approval, terms and rates are determined by the financial institution, not by Bono.
