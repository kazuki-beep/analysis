Case study · Beverages

Grupo Modelo

A pioneer in its commitment to decarbonization. Since 2022, Bono has accompanied Grupo Modelo on its journey toward an ambitious goal: Net Zero in 2040.

The challenge

A Net Zero target that could not be reached alone.

Grupo Modelo set an ambitious commitment: Net Zero by 2040. For a beverage company, that target cannot be met inside its own plants. The largest part of the footprint sits upstream, in a supply chain spread across sectors and borders — and none of it could be managed without first being measured.

The starting point

Understand where suppliers actually stood.

The work began with a deep understanding of the current sustainability status of Grupo Modelo's suppliers — across paper, cardboard, plastic, sugar, rice and corn, operating in seven Latin American countries. Sectors with very different data maturity, and very different starting positions.

The Bono solution

Baselines first, for every supplier.

Bono worked with those suppliers to measure their carbon footprint and establish an emissions baseline for 2022 and 2023. That step did more than give each supplier clarity about its own environmental impact: it allowed Grupo Modelo to see, for the first time, where the critical emissions in its supply chain actually were.

The roadmap

Prioritize by impact, not by convenience.

With that information, the teams began outlining a decarbonization roadmap that prioritized the areas with the greatest potential for impact. Bono has worked hand in hand with Grupo Modelo to explore solutions ranging from on-site renewable energy — such as solar panel installation — to the purchase of renewable energy.

Why it matters

One company changing an ecosystem.

Grupo Modelo's path toward Net Zero shows how collaboration and a commitment to innovation can transform not only a company, but an entire ecosystem of suppliers around it. Each supplier that measures and reduces carries that capability into every other customer it serves.
