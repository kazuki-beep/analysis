Glossary

The vocabulary, without the jargon tax.

Bono maintains a sustainability glossary. These are the entries that come up most often in enterprise conversations.

Carbon credit

A tradable instrument representing a tonne of CO₂e avoided or removed. Not a substitute for reduction.

Scope 1 / 2 / 3

Direct emissions, purchased energy, and everything in the value chain you influence but don't own.

GHG Protocol

The accounting standard most corporate carbon inventories and reporting frameworks are built on.

SBTi

The initiative that validates corporate emission-reduction targets against climate science.

$ / tCO₂e

Abatement cost. What one tonne of reduction costs through a given project — the number that ranks a roadmap.

Net Zero

Reducing emissions as far as possible and neutralizing only the residual. A trajectory, not a purchase.
